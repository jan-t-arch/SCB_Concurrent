此原型的未定問題Open questions in this prototype

以下項目仍需 BA／AC 確認。The following points still require BA/AC confirmation.

  1. AC11與AC14第8項的mandatory條件互相矛盾。
    AC11「Generic Reason for Concurrent Assessment」寫明：只有在用戶只選取部分Policy時，Generic Reason才是必填。換言之，如Situation 3內所有Policy均已選取，按AC11本身便不應要求此原因。
    但AC14第8項及mockup要求：Situation 3即使所有Policy均已選取，AC11仍須顯示並為必填。
    目前Demo採用：按AC14第8項及mockup處理；Situation 3一律要求完成AC11。
    待確認：正式系統應只在部分選取時要求AC11，還是在Situation 3任何選取組合下均要求AC11。     AC11 and AC14 item 8 define conflicting mandatory conditions.
    AC11, “Generic Reason for Concurrent Assessment”, states that the Generic Reason is mandatory only when the user selects some, but not all, Policies. Under AC11 alone, the reason should therefore not be required when all Policies in Situation 3 are selected.
    However, AC14 item 8 and the mockup require AC11 to remain visible and mandatory in Situation 3 even when all Policies are selected.
    Current Demo decision: Follow AC14 item 8 and the mockup; AC11 is mandatory for every Situation 3 selection combination.
    Confirmation required: Should production require AC11 only for partial selection, or for every selection combination in Situation 3?
  2. 未定：如已產生PDF後、完成簽署／提交前，PDF內唯一被選取的Policy被其他Application刪除，正式系統應容許繼續使用舊PDF，還是要求作廢並按最新Concurrent資料重新產生？目前Demo容許繼續簽署及提交已凍結PDF。Open: If the only selected policy in a generated PDF is deleted by another application before signing or submission is completed, should production allow the frozen PDF to continue, or require invalidation and regeneration from the latest Concurrent data? The current demo allows the existing frozen PDF to be signed and submitted.

1. Scope & Definitions1. Scope & Definitions

以下名詞適用於本頁所有requirements、decision tables及state transitions。The following terms apply to every requirement, decision table and state transition on this page.

名詞Term定義Definition
Application一份獨立保險申請記錄，擁有自己的Quotation、section completion、PDF、signing及submission狀態。An independent insurance application record with its own Quotation, section-completion, PDF, signing and submission states.
Current Application用戶目前正在處理及準備產生PDF的Application。The Application currently being processed and prepared for PDF generation.
Concurrent Group因同一客戶、同一銷售人員及同一簽署日期而被系統視為相關的一組Applications。A set of Applications treated as related because they share the same client, sales staff and signing date.
Active Policy目前存在於其Owner Application之有效Quotation內，並符合auto-search資格的Policy。A Policy that currently exists in a valid Quotation owned by its Application and is eligible for auto-search.
Auto-searched Policy系統在refresh checkpoint按Concurrent條件搜尋所得的Active Policy。An Active Policy returned by the system at a refresh checkpoint using the Concurrent matching conditions.
Manual PolicyAuto-search未能找到、由用戶在Concurrent Group層級補充的Policy；不屬於任何Application Quotation。A Policy missed by auto-search and supplied by the user at Concurrent Group level; it is not owned by an Application Quotation.
Effective Included Policy List本次簽署、Total／Max及PDF實際採用的Policies：Option A只包括Current Policy；Option B包括已選auto-searched Policies及Complete、auto-saved Manual Policies。The Policies actually used for the signing batch, Total/Max and PDF: Current Policy only for Option A; selected auto-searched Policies plus Complete, auto-saved Manual Policies for Option B.
Acknowledged Snapshot最近一次於Concurrent Entry reload後通過normal validation並獲系統確認的Concurrent Group Policy List及重要資料版本，用於下一checkpoint比較變更。The most recent Concurrent Group Policy List and material-data version acknowledged by the system after a Concurrent-entry reload passes normal validation, used for comparison at the next checkpoint.
Outstanding PDF已產生但尚未因Submit、Void PDF或Delete其未提交Owner Application而解除的PDF。A generated PDF that has not been cleared through Submit, Void PDF or deletion of its unsubmitted owner Application.
Pending Policy ChangeOutstanding PDF鎖定期間偵測到、但尚未載入凍結Concurrent Group的最新Policy差異。A latest Policy difference detected while an Outstanding PDF locks the Group but not yet admitted into the frozen Concurrent Group.

CS-MAN-001Manual Policy用戶可補充auto-search遺漏的Policy。The user may supply a Policy missed by auto-search.所需Policy未出現在auto-searched list。A required Policy is absent from the auto-searched list.系統必須在Concurrent Group層級建立Manual Policy。Complete要求Type及Premium > 0；Regular另要求pt > 0並固定Monthly Frequency。The system shall create a Manual Policy at Concurrent Group level.
