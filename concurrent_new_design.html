1. Scope & Definitions1. Scope & Definitions

以下名詞適用於本頁所有requirements、decision tables及state transitions。The following terms apply to every requirement, decision table and state transition on this page.

名詞Term定義Definition
Application一份獨立保險申請記錄，擁有自己的Quotation、section completion、PDF、signing及submission狀態。An independent insurance application record with its own Quotation, section-completion, PDF, signing and submission states.
Current Application用戶目前正在處理及準備產生PDF的Application。The Application currently being processed and prepared for PDF generation.
Concurrent Group因同一客戶、同一銷售人員及同一簽署日期而被系統視為相關的一組Applications。A set of Applications treated as related because they share the same client, sales staff and signing date.
Active Policy目前存在於其Owner Application之有效Quotation內、已完成所有適用必填資料，並符合目前Customer／P／LP identity auto-search範圍的Policy。單純空白Application、Incomplete Quotation或Party mismatch不屬有效auto-search Policy。A Policy that exists in a valid Quotation owned by its Application, has completed every applicable mandatory field, and matches the current Customer/P/LP identity auto-search scope. Riders do not enter the Policy List, Case A/B/C, Group Applicable PT, Aggregate Premium, Candidate Maximum Pool or PDF. Case A/B/C, Group Applicable PT and Aggregate Premium are determined by the Effective Included List. The Candidate Maximum Pool contains only selected system-detected Basic Plans in that Included List. A Complete Manual Policy may affect Case, PT and Aggregate but creates no Candidate. An unselected system-detected Policy participates in neither side of the formula, and its Premium, PT, D5/D6 and other calculation content do not trigger a Calculation-Factor ⚠; however, its addition, removal, eligibility change or stable Policy-identity change remains a Policy List Change.
Acknowledged Snapshot最近一次於Concurrent Entry reload後通過normal validation並獲系統確認的Concurrent Group Source Revision、Policy List及重要資料版本，作為下一次變更的比較基準。The most recent Concurrent Group Source Revision, Policy List and material-data version acknowledged by the system after a Concurrent-entry reload passes normal validation, used as the baseline for the next change.
Outstanding PDF已產生但尚未因Submit、Void PDF或Delete其未提交Owner Application而解除的PDF。A generated PDF that has not been cleared through Submit, Void PDF or deletion of its unsubmitted owner Application.
Group Change LatchOutstanding PDF週期內，偵測到適用的Policy List Change，或Option 2／Yes下Selected／Included Policy的Calculation Factor Change時，在全Group設置的狀態。Policy List Change不受Q1答案或selection影響；Calculation Factor Change只適用於Option 2／Yes及Selected／Included Policies。Latch一經設置便保持至最後一份Outstanding PDF解除。A Group-wide state set during an Outstanding-PDF cycle when the system detects an applicable Policy List Change, or a Calculation-Factor Change for a Selected/Included Policy under Option 2/Yes. A Policy List Change is independent of the Q1 answer and selection; a Calculation-Factor Change applies only under Option 2/Yes and to Selected/Included Policies. Once set, the latch remains until the last Outstanding PDF is cleared.
